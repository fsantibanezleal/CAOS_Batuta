- Area `context`: `context build` renders a knowledge manifest (C12) into a byte-stable `INDEX.md`, one path-scoped stub per `load: path` unit and the always-loaded `CORE.md`, and refuses output over the manifest budget; `context render` fills a template's `{{name}}` placeholders; `context check` fails when the user core, unscoped rules, the project CLAUDE.md chain or a memory index exceed the C01 budgets or Claude Code's 200-line or 25 KB MEMORY.md cap, naming the largest sections and the layer to demote them to; `context measure` prints the session-start payload breakdown (bytes divided by 4 is the token estimate).
- Area `memory`: `memory index` regenerates a project's MEMORY.md as pinned lines plus topic pages from a C13 topics file, keeps old titles and hooks, reports DANGLING entries and MISSING pins, refuses an index over the C01 or C14 budget, and is idempotent.
- Shared module `batuta_lib/render.py` (placeholder rendering that refuses leftovers, atomic LF writes, NEW/SAME/CHANGED status) and `batuta_lib/memory_index.py` (the index generator).
- Templates: `templates/user/CLAUDE.template.md` (generic always-loaded user core, under 7000 bytes rendered) with on-demand reference files (deep work, automation, disks, agentic orchestration) that each state a `Read when:` trigger, and `templates/knowledge/` (a sample manifest with units).
